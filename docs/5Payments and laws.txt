the big picture:

1. **Read and understand** the Moldovan Civil Code articles related to:

   * **Service Contracts (Art. 1194–1199)** — your app’s relationship with contractors.
   * **Contractual Obligations (Art. 1165–1168)** — general duties and rights under contracts.
   * **Mandate / Intermediary agreements (Art. 1190–1193)** — if you handle payments as escrow or agent.

2. **Register your company as an SRL** — a limited liability company in Moldova. This will be your legal entity operating the platform.

3. **Contractors: two main groups:**

   * **I.P.s (Întreprinzător Individual):** Registered freelancers who invoice you and pay their own taxes.
   * **Non-registered individuals:** Work occasionally, get paid by you, and are responsible for declaring income/taxes themselves (or sometimes don’t).

4. **Big platforms like Glovo, Bolt, and Duck** operate with this model:

   * They register as companies (SRLs or bigger)
   * Contractors work as I.P.s or non-registered freelancers
   * Platforms act as intermediaries and take commissions (often with escrow systems)
   * Contractors handle their own tax filings




What to read:

Art. 1194–1199 — Contract de prestări servicii (Service Contract)
Art. 1165–1168 — Obligații contractuale (Contractual obligations)
Art. 1190–1193 — Mandate / Intermediary agreements (if you're holding money for someone else)

SRL checklist:

| ✅ Step | Description                                                                                |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1️⃣    | Choose company name (verify it’s unique at [asp.gov.md](https://asp.gov.md))               |
| 2️⃣    | Choose legal address (can be your apartment or lawyer’s address)                           |
| 3️⃣    | Choose activity code (CAEM) — most likely `6311` (Web platforms) or `6202` (IT consulting) |
| 4️⃣    | Choose administrator (your name or a partner)                                              |
| 5️⃣    | Prepare charter (standard one is okay at start)                                            |
| 6️⃣    | Register online via [MSign](https://servicii.gov.md) or at ASP physically                  |
| 7️⃣    | Get company stamp (optional but useful)                                                    |
| 8️⃣    | Open bank account (e.g., maib, Victoriabank, Mobias)                                       |
| 9️⃣    | Register for taxes at SFS (Serviciul Fiscal de Stat)                                       |
| 🔟    | Start issuing invoices and operate legally                                                 |





Example of worker when works:

CASE A: Cleaner is registered as an I.P. (freelancer / “Întreprinzător Individual”)
They:
Receive 700 MDL
Issue you or the client an invoice
Declare it in their accounting
Pay ~7% tax on that money (or more, depending on regime)
✅ All legal, clean.
❌ But most people don’t want to register an I.P. — too much work.

CASE B: Cleaner is not registered and just receives money
This is what happens 90% of the time in Glovo, Bolt, Fiverr, and probably your app too.
They:
Receive 700 MDL to their card or cash
Don’t register anywhere
Don’t pay tax unless the tax office finds out and asks
🔹 Legally, they are supposed to declare that money as "occasional income" and pay income tax.
🔹 In practice, they almost never do.
✅ You are not responsible for this.


Terms of Service, clarifying the contractor’s responsibility for taxes and registration:

**Responsabilitatea fiscală și înregistrarea contractorilor**
1. Contractorii care oferă servicii prin intermediul platformei sunt responsabili pentru înregistrarea 
lor fiscală și pentru plata tuturor impozitelor și contribuțiilor sociale aferente veniturilor obținute.
2. Platforma funcționează ca un intermediar și nu este angajatorul contractorilor.
3. Contractorii trebuie să fie înregistrati legal (de exemplu, ca Întreprinzător Individual) 
conform legislației în vigoare și să emită facturi sau alte documente fiscale atunci când este cazul.
4. Platforma reține un comision pentru facilitarea tranzacțiilor între clienți și contractorii care prestează serviciile.
5. Contractorii sunt singurii responsabili pentru respectarea obligațiilor fiscale, iar platforma nu răspunde pentru eventualele datorii fiscale ale acestora.

**Налоговые обязательства и регистрация подрядчиков**
1. Подрядчики, предоставляющие услуги через платформу, несут ответственность за свою налоговую регистрацию 
и уплату всех налогов и социальных взносов, связанных с полученными доходами.
2. Платформа выступает посредником и не является работодателем подрядчиков.
3. Подрядчики обязаны быть официально зарегистрированы (например, как индивидуальные предприниматели) 
в соответствии с действующим законодательством и при необходимости выставлять счета или другие налоговые документы.
4. Платформа удерживает комиссию за посредничество при проведении сделок между клиентами и подрядчиками.
5. Подрядчики самостоятельно несут ответственность за выполнение налоговых обязательств, и платформа не отвечает за возможные налоговые задолженности подрядчиков.
